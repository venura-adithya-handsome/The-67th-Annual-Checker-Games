# The-67th-Annual-Checker-Games
It's the 67th annual Checker Games,
In the year 3067, 2 kids from each
district of the Czech Republic are 
used for the Capitol's entertainment

They fight to the death, 24 in and 1 out,
Your odds are slim, but you know 
deep in your heart, that your chance 
of winning will never be 0.

Happy Checker Games, 
and may the odds be ever in your favor!
